# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026._

This policy explains what the Ascend app ("the app", "we") does with your data. Ascend is a working title; the app's displayed name may change.

**In short:** we don't collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, no server and no analytics, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The System's coaching messages are written on your iPhone from built-in templates. Chronicle cards are made on your iPhone and go only where you send them. You can delete everything at any time.

## What stays on your device

- Your training log: Trials, every set you log (reps, load, reps in reserve), exercise swaps, working loads, XP, Levels, Training Weeks, Story progress, Side Missions (Mobility Drills done and your Recovery Check ratings) and your Class.
- Your Scan answers: after a Trial, how each muscle it trained felt (Easy, Right or Too much), and at the start of the next Trial that trains it, whether it is still sore (No, A little or Yes). The app works out each muscle's Scan state (Primed, Recovering or Strained) and its sets for the next Training Week from them on the device. These are training states, not health or medical data.
- Your avatar and the Title you wear. Mastery levels, earned Titles, Confrontation results, exercise history and Chronicles are worked out from your training log on the device.
- The System's messages (briefings, debriefs and story beats). In this version they are written on your iPhone from templates built into the app; nothing is sent to an AI service.
- Your settings, such as your load unit (kg or lb), the rest-over notification switch, form cues and whether the story shows during rests.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is not synced to iCloud and there is no server to send it to. It is included in your device backups as normal for app data.

**Apple Health:** if you allow it, the app saves each finished Trial to Apple Health as a strength-training workout. The app only writes workouts; it never reads your Health data, and Health data never leaves your device because of the app.

**Notifications:** at your first rest timer with the app open, iOS asks whether the app may send you notifications. If you allow it, the app schedules one local notification on your iPhone for the end of each rest ("Rest over", the exercise, the set number and the next target), with a Log set button. It is scheduled and delivered by iOS on your device: nothing is sent to us or anyone else, the app uses no push notifications and has no device token. Turn it off in Settings (Rest-over notification) or in iPhone Settings > Notifications.

**Lock Screen:** while a Trial runs, its Live Activity shows the current exercise, set and rest timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and lets you log a set or skip a rest there. This is shown by iOS on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to send it.

### 1. Crash reports

If you've agreed to share analytics with Apple (iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with us through Xcode and App Store Connect. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy. TestFlight testers may also choose to send feedback and screenshots through TestFlight.

### 2. Chronicle cards (only when you share or save one)

A Chronicle is a picture card summarising a Training Week or a Chapter. The app draws it on your iPhone from your training log. By default it shows only relative numbers (gains in percent, Mastery levels, counts); a "Show loads" switch, off each time you open the share screen, adds your top lift's load. A card never shows your name, email or Health data.

The app never uploads a card and doesn't record whether you shared one. When you tap Share, the iOS share sheet sends the image wherever you choose, such as Messages or another app; from then on, that app's or person's privacy practices apply. If you choose **Save Image**, iOS asks once for permission to add photos to your library. This is add-only access: the app can save the cards you choose but can't see, read or change any of your photos.

### 3. Links you open

The Sources screen links to the published research behind the training rules. Tapping one opens it in Safari, outside the app, under the website's own privacy policy.

## What we never do

- No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
- No use of Health or fitness data for advertising or marketing.
- No accounts, names, emails, passwords or identifiers of any kind in this version.

## Who processes data for us

No one. The app uses no servers, AI providers or other third-party services, and no SDK that collects data. Apple distributes the app and TestFlight and may share crash reports with us as described above, under Apple's policies.

## Delete my data

In the app: Settings > Data & privacy > **Delete my data**. After you confirm, the app deletes everything it stored on your device (training log, XP, Side Missions and recovery ratings, Scan answers, avatar and Titles, the System's messages, settings), ends any running Live Activity, cancels any pending rest-over notification and returns to its first screen. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Workouts the app saved to Apple Health stay in Health, and Chronicle cards you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them; delete them there if you want. Deleting the app also deletes its on-device data.

## Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold no personal data about you: everything is on your device, where you can see it in the app and remove it with "Delete my data". For any other request, contact us.

## Changes

We'll update this policy when the app's data practices change and change the date above, before the change ships. Material changes will be announced in the app or the TestFlight release notes.
